# Approved Resume Verbs

This is the single source of truth for resume bullet starter verbs.

## Approved starter verbs

Ownership:
- led
- owned
- defined
- drove
- launched
- shipped

Strategy:
- prioritized
- roadmapped
- scoped
- aligned
- pitched

Research:
- uncovered
- synthesized
- validated
- reframed
- surfaced

Impact:
- improved
- reduced
- increased
- accelerated
- scaled

Execution:
- coordinated
- partnered
- delivered
- iterated
- unblocked

## Banned starter phrases

- conducted
- supported
- assisted
- helped
- participated
- worked on
- was responsible for
- involved in

Any generated bullet that starts with a banned phrase fails validation.

# Product Manager HR Gate

Use this when the target role is Product Manager, Product Owner, Growth PM,
Senior PM, Lead PM, or a product role at a named company.

---

## What recruiters screen for

For PM roles, ATS keywords are not enough. Recruiters look for proof that the
candidate can turn user/business problems into product decisions and shipped
outcomes.

Strong PM evidence:

- Owned or influenced roadmap, backlog, prioritization, PRD, or feature scope
- Aligned engineering, design, data, business, marketing, operations, or regional teams
- Used research/analytics to decide what to build or improve
- Shipped or changed a feature, flow, experiment, onboarding, pricing, search,
  recommendation, localization, marketplace, or AI assistant experience
- Moved product metrics: activation, conversion, retention, CSAT, NPS, rating,
  adoption, GMV, revenue, cost, MAU, bookings, users, investors

Weak PM evidence:

- "Conducted research" without decision or product change
- "Supported product development" without ownership or output
- "Used Figma/Jira/Power BI" without what was decided or shipped
- Entrepreneur revenue that does not connect to product, marketplace, users,
  prioritization, or product operations

---

## Senior PM reality check

Senior PM resumes usually need evidence of at least two:

- Owned a roadmap area or major product surface
- Prioritized trade-offs across customer, business, and engineering constraints
- Defined PRD/requirements and aligned cross-functional delivery
- Made or influenced launch decisions
- Led experiments or product iteration loops
- Owned metrics and improved them
- Mentored or coordinated other PM/research/product stakeholders

If these are missing, recommend targeting Product Manager, Associate PM, Product
Researcher/Product Strategy, or a bridge role instead of Senior PM. If the user
insists on Senior PM, ask for missing evidence before generating.

---

## Target-company logic

For named-company resumes:

- Expand the most domain-relevant experience even if it was short.
- Move unrelated current roles lower or reframe them as transferable leadership.
- Remove or minimize sections that create doubt about commitment or fit.
- Tailor summary and top bullets to the target company's domain.

Example for Traveloka:

Prioritize travel-tech, user onboarding, multilingual/localization, AI travel
advisor, search/discovery, booking flow, regional user behavior, marketplace
dynamics, experimentation, and customer satisfaction metrics.

---

## Intake probes

Ask one at a time:

- "What product decision changed because of this research?"
- "What feature, flow, experiment, or product surface did this affect?"
- "Who used the research: PM, designer, engineer, leadership, business team?"
- "How many users/interviews/survey responses/events/segments were involved?"
- "What metric moved: CSAT, conversion, activation, rating, retention, MAU?"
- "Did you own the PRD, prioritization, roadmap, backlog, or launch decision?"
- "For an active unrelated business, should it be framed as full-time, advisory,
  paused, or moved below product experience?"

---

## Resume writing rules

- Do not write aspirational phrases like "targeting Senior PM".
- Do not lead with entrepreneur identity unless the target role values founder
  PM/operator experience and the work connects to product leadership.
- Put the most relevant PM/travel-tech proof first, not necessarily the newest
  job.
- If a role title is Researcher, bullets must show research-to-decision impact.
- If a role title is Entrepreneur, bullets must show PM-transferable ownership:
  customer discovery, market validation, pricing, product operations,
  partnerships, revenue, team leadership, or marketplace/supply-demand logic.

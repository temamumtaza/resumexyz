# User Journey: Before vs After
**Scenario:** Mid-career PM making a move to Senior PM at a startup  
**Shows:** How improvements compound to make experience feel "lebih dekat"

---

## Phase 1: Target Discovery

### BEFORE
```
You: "I want to make a senior PM resume"

Agent: "What kind of role are you going for?
        Even a rough idea helps me shape everything from the summary
        to which skills to highlight."

You: [answer]

Agent: [shows 3 generic questions in sequence]

⏰ Feeling: Interrogated. Generic form.
🤔 Clarity: "Where are we in this process?" — No idea.
```

---

### AFTER
```
You: "I want to make a senior PM resume"

Agent: "Let's start with direction. What's the next move you're 
        targeting — are you leveling up, jumping into something new, 
        or moving at the same level to a different company?"

You: "Leveling up to Senior PM at a startup"

Agent: "You're targeting Senior PM — that's ambitious. I can help.
        Here's the bar: hiring managers will need proof of roadmap 
        ownership, shipped product decisions, and metric movement.
        
        Do you have that story in your background?"

You: "Yeah, I've shipped features and owned their impact"

Agent: ✓ Phase 1 complete: Your target is clear
       [Senior PM at startup, growth focus]
       
       ⏳ Phase 2 next: Work experience deep-dive
       [4-5 questions to capture your strongest roles]

⏰ Feeling: Understood. Respected. Know what's coming.
🤔 Clarity: "We're building toward [target]. Halfway through intake."
```

**Difference:** From interrogation → dialogue. Progress visible.

---

## Phase 2: Experience Collection

### BEFORE
```
Agent: "For each role, send anything you remember:
        - Job title, company, location, dates
        - How many people you led or coordinated, if any
        - Whether you were the owner, co-owner, or contributor
        - Concrete decisions that changed because of your contribution
        [10-item list]
        
        Any format is fine. Rough notes are enough."

You: [types some details, feels like a form]

Agent: [asks follow-up] "This still reads like a job description. 
        What is one specific thing you personally handled, improved, 
        built, analyzed, coordinated, sold, launched, fixed, or delivered?"

You: [feels judged. Your answer was the problem?]

⏰ Feeling: Am I doing this right? Defensive.
📝 Tone: Feels like compliance checking.
```

---

### AFTER
```
Agent: "Now the most important part: your experience.
        
        For each role (start with the most recent), share anything 
        you remember:
        - What you accomplished, not just what the job was
        - Concrete outcomes: numbers, shipped products, processes improved
        - Tools and methods you used
        - People you led, trained, or coordinated
        - One thing you're proud of from that role
        
        Rough notes are fine — jot them however is fastest. 
        I'll turn them into polished resume bullets."

You: [shares details in own voice. Feels like story, not form]

Agent: [acknowledges what you said first]
        "I see you shipped the checkout flow. Now the crucial part
        for Senior PM — what changed in the product *because* of you?
        Did conversion go up? Did users have a smoother experience?
        What's the business impact?"

You: [feels supported to dig deeper, not judged]

✓ Phase 2A complete: Contact info captured
✓ Phase 2B complete: [3] roles documented
  - [Spotify (2018-2020)]
  - [Uber Eats (2020-2023)]
  - [Current startup (2023-Present)]

⏰ Feeling: My story matters. Supported in digging deep.
📝 Tone: Partnership on evidence building.
```

**Difference:** From form-filling → story mining. Feels collaborative.

## Phase 3: Analysis

### BEFORE
```
Agent: "Before I generate, I see screening risks:
        1. Your Senior PM target — lacking roadmap ownership evidence
        2. Current freelance work — creates commitment risk
        
        How should I handle this?"

You: [feels defensive. "Risks"? "How should I handle"?]

⏰ Feeling: Failed a gate. Worried about output quality.
😟 Trust: Just told me I'm not ready. Why generate at all?
```

---

### AFTER
```
Agent: "Before we go to final resume, let's address two things
        hiring managers will notice. These aren't deal-breakers —
        we just need to frame them clearly.
        
        🎯 Your Senior PM ambition — I need proof of ownership:
        
        Looking back, did you ever own a roadmap area, prioritize
        a backlog, define a PRD, align design-engineering, or make
        a launch decision? If you've got one solid example, that
        lifts the whole resume.
        
        🎯 Your current freelance work — is this full-time or more
        advisory/part-time? Let's make the commitment level clear
        so recruiters don't wonder about your availability.
        
        For the roadmap question, what's the strongest example?"

You: [given specific, actionable pathways. Feels solvable]

✓ Phase 3 complete: Analysis passed
  Readiness score: 82/100
  Ready to generate: YES
  
  Your strongest angles:
  • Shipped 3 product launches that moved core metrics
  • Led cross-functional alignment on major checkout redesign
  
  Next step: I'll build your resume now.

⏰ Feeling: You understand my gaps AND my strengths.
😊 Trust: Confident in what's coming.
```

**Difference:** From warnings → partnership. From risk → opportunity.

## Phase 5: Iteration

### BEFORE
```
You: "The summary sounds too corporate. Can it be more startup-focused?"

Agent: "I'll rewrite it. Let me make it more startup-focused."
       [updates file, re-delivers]
       
       "Updated:
        • Summary rewritten"

You: [opens PDF, re-reads entire summary]
     "Hmm, is this better? Hard to tell what changed..."

⏰ Feeling: Did you actually address my feedback?
😕 Trust: Can't see the shift.
```

---

### AFTER
```
You: "The summary sounds too corporate. Can it be more startup-focused?"

Agent: "Got it — let me understand. What's the startup vibe you want?
        Scrappy? Metrics-obsessed? Bias toward shipping? Give me the tone."

You: "Scrappy, shipping-focused, less formal"

Agent: [updates and delivers with proof]

       "Here's the revised Summary:
       
        ❌ OLD: Shipping-focused product leader with 5+ years scaling
               platform adoption and driving cross-functional product
               decisions at high-growth SaaS...
       
        ✅ NEW: PM who ships. Shipped 3 major features; each moved core
               metrics. Comfortable in chaos, biased toward testing and
               iterating, and unafraid to kill things that don't work.
               5+ years at Spotify, Uber Eats, and early-stage startups."

You: [sees both versions. YES, way better. Tone shift is clear.]

⏰ Feeling: You nailed it. This IS more me.
😊 Trust: Feedback loop works. You get it.
```

**Difference:** From black-box revision → transparent before/after. Trust built.

## Phase 6: Final Wrap

### BEFORE
```
Agent: "Your resume is finalized.

  Before submitting:
  
  File format: Use .docx for ATS...
  File name: FirstName-LastName...
  LinkedIn: Update your profile...
  Per-application tailoring: Before each submission...
  
  Good luck."

You: [reads as homework checklist. Feels like you're leaving.]

⏰ Feeling: OK, I'm on my own now.
👋 Tone: Goodbye.
```

---

### AFTER
```
Agent: "You're ready to ship.

  Before you hit send, one final pass:
  
  📋 Format:
     - Use the .docx for ATS systems
     - Keep the PDF for your visual reference or screen sharing
  
  📝 File name:
     - FirstName-LastName-Resume.docx
     - Clean name = professionalism signal to recruiters
  
  🔗 Sync your LinkedIn:
     - Match titles, dates, companies exactly
     - Recruiters always cross-check — inconsistencies are instant red flags
  
  🎯 Per-application edge:
     - For each application, skim the job posting's Skills section
     - Add 3-5 exact keywords to your Skills section
     - This is the single highest-impact thing you can do
  
---
  
  Questions before you submit? Happy to revise one more time or
  answer anything about your positioning.
  
  You've got this."

You: [feels like real advice from someone who knows startups]

⏰ Feeling: You're still here if I need you.
🚀 Tone: Partnership through the finish line.
```

**Difference:** From dismissal → continued support. Door stays open.

## User's Emotional Arc

```
BEFORE:
┌─ Start: Hopeful
│  ├─ Phase 1: Confused (generic questions)
│  ├─ Phase 2: Defensive (judged on answers)
│  ├─ Phase 3: Worried (told about "risks")
│  ├─ Phase 4: Uncertain (is this me?)
│  ├─ Phase 5: Skeptical (can't see the changes)
│  └─ End: Alone (left to figure it out)
└─ Net feeling: Processed, not supported

AFTER:
┌─ Start: Hopeful
│  ├─ Phase 1: Understood (you get my move)
│  ├─ Phase 2: Supported (mining my story)
│  ├─ Phase 3: Partnered (let's solve this together)
│  ├─ Phase 4: Validated (yes, that IS me)
│  ├─ Phase 5: Confident (proof you nailed it)
│  └─ End: Empowered (you're with me in this)
└─ Net feeling: Heard, understood, supported

Emotional lift: Processing → Partnership
              Transactional → Relational
              Distant → Dekat
```

## The Compounding Effect

Each improvement alone is small. But **together**, they transform the experience:

✅ Progress tracker = "I'm moving forward"  
✅ Context before asking = "You understand what I need"  
✅ Red flag partnership = "You're on my side"  
✅ Personal callback = "You listened to me"  
✅ Before/after proof = "I can trust you"  

**Result:** User feels **seen, guided, partnered, understood** at every step.

That's "lebih dekat" — closer, more personal, more human.
